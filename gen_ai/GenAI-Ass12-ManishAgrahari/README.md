## Dataset

**Dataset Name:** Sales Dataset

**Source:** Kaggle

**Kaggle Link:** https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/vinothkannaece/sales-dataset

**File Used:** sales_data.csv

The same dataset is used for all tasks from Task 1 to Task 8.


## Dataset Description

The dataset contains sales information such as sales amount, quantity sold,
sales representative, region, product category, customer type,
payment method and sales channel.

### Important Columns

- `Sales_Amount` - Amount of the sale
- `Quantity_Sold` - Quantity of products sold
- `Sales_Rep` - Sales representative
- `Region` - Sales region
- `Product_Category` - Category of the product
- `Unit_Cost` - Cost of one unit
- `Unit_Price` - Selling price of one unit
- `Customer_Type` - Type of customer
- `Discount` - Discount given on the sale
- `Payment_Method` - Method used for payment
- `Sales_Channel` - Sales channel used


## Libraries Used

- Pandas - Used for loading and exploring the dataset
- Seaborn - Used for data visualization
- Matplotlib - Used for displaying the plots
